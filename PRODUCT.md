# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two groups share one app.

- **Residents** of a flood-prone Malaysian township, many of them older, checking a phone during or before heavy rain. Often outdoors, in rain or bright sun, on mobile data and low battery, sometimes frightened. Jobs: know whether rain is coming, report flooding with a photo, find a safe point or road to avoid, track their report, ask Titis (the chat mascot) a question.
- **Operators** on the township team at a desktop, ranking zones by risk and dispatching crews.

English and Bahasa Malaysia throughout.

## Product Purpose

SmartFlow is a flood early-warning prototype. It turns resident photo reports, drain sensors, and live weather into clear advice for residents and a ranked work list for operators. Success: a worried resident knows within five seconds whether they are safe and what to do next, and can send a report in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trusted, local. A steady neighbour, not a siren. Plain short sentences. The sky and the weather set the mood of the screen; colour turns loud only when there is real danger. Friendly through Titis and a personal greeting, never jokey in a crisis.

References: Apple Weather (sky-driven background, big temperature, hourly strip), Google Maps and Waze (map-first, floating controls, live dot), Linear and Notion (quiet precision, typography doing the work), Grab and Touch 'n Go (familiar Malaysian super-app greeting header and quick actions).

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: gradient hero metrics, rows of identical icon cards.
- Panic red everywhere. Red is reserved for danger and life-critical lines.
- Glassy decorative blur, neon, dark-mode-for-cool.

## Design Principles

1. **Five-second answer.** Every resident screen leads with "am I OK, and what now".
2. **Calm until it is not.** Quiet surfaces by default so warnings stand out when they appear.
3. **The sky is the status.** Live weather drives the atmosphere at the top of the app.
4. **Built for wet hands and old eyes.** Large text, large targets, one clear primary action.
5. **Never invent safety facts.** Life-critical lines are fixed; AI only assists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum. Older residents: base text 17px or larger, tap targets 48px, high contrast that holds in direct sunlight. Status always shown as text plus colour. Respect reduced motion (in-app toggle and OS setting); keep motion light to save battery. Full English and Bahasa Malaysia.
